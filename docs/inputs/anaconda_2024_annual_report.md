# Anaconda Automotive Holdings
## 2024 Annual Report

![Anaconda Automotive Logo]

### Letter to Our Investors

Dear Valued Investors and Stakeholders,

As I reflect on 2024, I am proud to share another remarkable year of growth and resilience for Anaconda Automotive Holdings. In a year marked by both challenges and opportunities in the automotive retail sector, our strategic focus on Asian automotive brands and our commitment to exceptional customer service has continued to drive our success.

Our network of seven dealerships across the Eastern and Midwest United States achieved record-breaking sales of 12,847 units, representing a 14.3% increase from 2023. This growth was particularly noteworthy given the industry-wide supply chain pressures and shifting consumer preferences we navigated throughout the year.

Key highlights from 2024 include:
- Launch of our innovative "Anaconda Digital" platform, enhancing our online sales capabilities
- Expansion of our high-end sedan portfolio, which saw a 22% growth in sales
- Achievement of industry-leading customer satisfaction scores across all locations
- Successful implementation of our sustainability initiatives, reducing our carbon footprint by 15%

Looking ahead to 2025, we remain focused on strategic growth opportunities while maintaining our commitment to operational excellence. The automotive industry continues to evolve rapidly, and Anaconda is well-positioned to capitalize on emerging trends in electrification and digital retail.

I want to thank our dedicated team members, loyal customers, and you, our investors, for your continued support and trust in Anaconda Automotive Holdings.

Sincerely,

Sarah Chen
President & CEO
Anaconda Automotive Holdings
